# Content And Outline

Create `requirements.md` after intake with these fields, in this order:

```text
purpose, audience, setting, duration, takeaway, must-include,
must-exclude, sources, sensitivity, deadline, visual constraints, output path.
```

Create `content-source.md` only after requirements and the research decision are approved. It is a full material library, never direct slide markup. Keep complete arguments, chapter logic, cases, data and qualifications, optional content, source markers, speaking prompts, and rejected content with reasons. When research is selected, add the useful findings here and in the source manifest before they reach a slide. When fact-checking is selected, preserve the original claim with its verification note or correction. When research is declined, record that choice and do not invent external support. Interviews are optional input only; do not assume interviews.

Create `outline.md` only after content approval. Use these fields for every slide:

```text
id, title, pageType, coreMessage, screenText, visualForm,
contentSources, assetSources, transition, included, reviewStatus.
```

Give every slide one core message. Split dense material naturally instead of forcing it onto one slide or imposing a fixed total slide limit. Keep full explanations in `content-source.md` or `speaker-notes.md`, not in dense screen text.

Maintain `materials/source-manifest.md` with this record for every asset:

```text
name, URL/path, retrievedAt, author/organization, license/status, slideUsage, finalStatus.
```
