# Research And Licensing

Research current data, industry cases, product capabilities, policies, standards, prices, exact quotations, or requested visual references before using them. Prefer official and primary sources, then research reports, papers, public data, clearly open repositories and design systems, and credible industry media. Treat other material only as leads.

Do not assume interviews. Use interview material only when supplied by the user or when the user explicitly requests interview analysis. Do not upload internal files to external services.

Use assets in this order: user-provided assets; authorized official assets; clearly open or licensed assets; self-made charts and diagrams; then user-approved generated images. Reject an asset with an unclear license, do not put it in a slide, and record it as unadopted.

Create `materials/source-manifest.md`. For each fact or asset, record:

```text
name, URL/path, retrievedAt, author/organization, license/status, slideUsage, finalStatus
```

Record the actual retrieval date, the source location, the intended slide use, and whether the item is finally adopted. Never copy an unlicensed commercial template or describe a visual reference as an open asset.
